# Fonts

The themes bundle these open fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1, from the Google Fonts project):
Young Serif, Instrument Sans (Paper); Bricolage Grotesque, Plus Jakarta Sans (Paper glass); Gloock, Figtree (Paper dark);
Unbounded, Outfit (Paper ink glass); Playfair Display, Sora (Paper sakura glass).
Variable fonts are used at their default weight.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts (folder `ofl`). Each font keeps its own copyright, see its folder there.
